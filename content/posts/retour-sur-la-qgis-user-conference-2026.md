<p>Oslandia took part (actively!) in the QGIS User Conference 2026, which was held in early October in Laax, Switzerland.</p>
<p>The teams led four workshops and talks on the first day:</p>
<ul>
<li>Workshop: ‘QGIS Expressions: from labels to geometry generators’ (by Benoit de Mezzo)</li>
<li>Deploying QGIS in large-scale IT systems – best practices (by Vincent Picavet)</li>
<li>Mental health and open source: a sensitive topic for engineers (by Vincent Picavet)</li>
<li>From users to contributors: rethinking participation in QGIS (by Jean Felder)</li>
</ul>
<p>The second day provided an opportunity to attend talks and forge links with the international community!</p>
