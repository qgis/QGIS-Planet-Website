<p>A few weeks ago, Oslandia organized a webinar/roundtable discussion titled<strong> “Creating QGIS Plugins in 2026: Challenges, Expectations, and Concerns!”</strong> open to the international developer community.</p>
<p>The goal was to <strong>share our experiences</strong> as QGIS plugin developers—including our objectives, habits, and challenges—as well as to discuss the available tools.</p>
<p>This discussion was also a great opportunity to share<strong> our feedback as developers</strong>, identify needs related to QGIS plugin development, and explore ways to make the most of our development work.</p>
<p>If you missed the session, you can find the recording here: <a href="https://s.zoom.us/m/bPlTUNXA2">https://s.zoom.us/m/bPlTUNXA2</a></p>
