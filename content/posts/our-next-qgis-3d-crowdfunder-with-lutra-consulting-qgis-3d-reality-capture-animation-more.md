<p>We are partnering again with our brilliant friends at <a href="https://www.lutraconsulting.co.uk/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Lutra Consulting</a> to develop some really neat features focusing on reality capture technology, improved 3D visuals, and upgrading animation support. <h2>How this crowdfunding campaign works</h2>
<p>If you are new to crowdfunding campaigns, we welcome you! Crowdfunding is a viable model when there are passionate users wanting to support development of features they see a need for. End users (or end-user organisations) pledge an amount they are willing to provide to the campaign, and if the goal is reached, that pledge becomes binding. If the target amount has not been reached then there will be no requirement to pay the pledged amount.</p>
<p>Here are the steps to pledge to the campaign:</p>
<ol>
<li>You <a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfCI0IRjYS9K6xH5h4fDDEr9DQWrn-Zz0xRBitd6wIZpmVSpA/viewform">pledge</a> the amount on the form – minimum €200 (~AUD$322) – see below <em>“Why a minimum €200?”</em></li>
<li>On December 7th, if we have reached our goal of €85 000, your pledge becomes binding and we will send you a payment link.</li>
<li>Once you have paid, you will receive a receipt (and our thanks).</li>
<li>We will then start developing the features for inclusion into QGIS in 2027 (versions 4.6, 4.8 and 4.10).</li>
</ol>
<h3>Why a minimum €200?</h3>
<p>Running a crowdfunding campaign is not just gloriously developing features – there is also a lot of administration and project management costs involved. We want to keep these costs down and devote as much of the funds as possible to the development work. Want to pledge less than €200? Reach out to your local <a href="https://www.qgis.org/community/groups/">QGIS User Group</a> or your <a href="https://www.osgeo.org/local-chapters/">OSGeo Local Chapter group</a> – they can help combine smaller community contributions to meet the minimum target together.</p>
